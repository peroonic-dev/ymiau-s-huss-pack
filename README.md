# 🐐 yMiau's Huss Pack

> The ultimate meme sound pack for Minecraft: Every sound effect is Huss!

A funny sound and particle resource pack built from clips of the streamer **yMiau**!
Compatible with Minecraft **1.21.11 through 26.3** (Vanilla, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Quilt).

**Made by Peroonic.**

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔊 **Every Sound Effect is Huss**: Footsteps, mobs, breaking blocks, item clicks, UI sounds, and weather.
- 🎯 **Pitch- & Length-Matched**: Sounds adapt to vanilla pitch and length (e.g. Goat Horns and Ghast screams are hilariously stretched).
- ✨ **Custom Huss Particles**: All particle textures replaced with transparent, color-matched "Huss" text.
- 🐐 **Goat Totem Pop**: The Totem of Undying triggers the screaming goat sound from yMiau's clip.
- 🎶 **Vanilla Music**: Background music and music discs remain 100% original vanilla Minecraft music.
- 📦 **Multi-Version Support**: Works from Minecraft 1.21.11 up to 26.3.

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Go to the [Releases](https://github.com/peroonic-dev/ymiau-s-huss-pack/releases) tab and download `Huss-Pack.zip`.
2. Move `Huss-Pack.zip` into your `.minecraft/resourcepacks` folder.
3. Open Minecraft, navigate to **Options -> Resource Packs**, and activate **yMiau's Huss Pack** (place it at the top).
